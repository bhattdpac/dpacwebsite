What I recommend we build

Instead of another WordPress site with static pages, I recommend turning deepakbhatt.dev into a research platform.

Technology stack:

Frontend: Next.js + Tailwind CSS
Backend: FastAPI
Database: PostgreSQL
Charts: Chart.js or Plotly
Markdown: MDX for research notes and weekly logs
Authentication: Private admin area for drafts, public view for approved content
Deployment: Your existing hosting or a VPS if needed

This architecture will let you publish progress with minimal effort while presenting a professional, evidence-based record of your research.

I would stop treating your website as a résumé.

Instead, treat it as a digital PhD thesis that evolves every week. By the time you submit your dissertation, your website will already contain your literature reviews, experiment logs, research journal, publications, and software artifacts—organized in a way that's useful for both your RDC committee and future employers.
