# Navigating the Landscape: Types of Blockchain Networks and Their Characteristics

This document explores different types of blockchain networks, their characteristics, and use cases in various applications.

## Key Knowledge Points

[[Public Blockchains]]
- Open access
- Decentralized governance
- Permissionless participation
- Transparent operations
- Cryptocurrency incentives

[[Private Blockchains]]
- Permissioned access
- Centralized governance
- Controlled participation
- Restricted visibility
- Enterprise applications

[[Consortium Blockchains]]
- Semi-decentralized nature
- Multi-organization governance
- Controlled access
- Shared responsibility
- Business networks

[[Hybrid Blockchains]]
- Combined features
- Flexible access control
- Customizable privacy
- Scalable solutions
- Enterprise adoption
