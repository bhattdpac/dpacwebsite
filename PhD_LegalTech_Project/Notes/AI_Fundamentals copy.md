# AI Fundamentals

Artificial Intelligence (AI) represents the capability of machines to perform tasks that typically require human intelligence. This field encompasses various technologies and approaches that enable computers to learn, reason, and make decisions. AI has evolved from simple rule-based systems to sophisticated machine learning and deep learning models that can process and analyze complex data patterns.
