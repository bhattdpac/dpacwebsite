# SaaS Conversion Roadmap (NBF Integrated)

## Background & Motivation
Transitioning the PhD framework into a scalable SaaS model aligned with India's National Blockchain Framework (NBF).

## NBF Product Alignment
The application will serve as a gateway for the following Live Chains:
1. **Document Chain:** For general legal agreement hashing and timestamping.
2. **Judiciary Chain:** For court evidence and legal process automation.
3. **Property Chain:** For automated land registry and sale deed execution.
4. **Certificate Chain:** For academic and professional credentialing.
5. **ICJS & Drug Logistics:** For inter-departmental data integrity and supply chain transparency.

## Phased Implementation Plan

### Phase 1: Multi-Tenancy & NBF Adapters
- **Org Model:** Multi-tenant support for law firms and institutions.
- **Vertical Skins:** Specialized UI for different NBF chains (Judiciary vs. Property).
- **Security:** PKI and e-Sign integration as per NBF standards.

### Phase 2: Billing & Subscriptions
- Stripe integration for professional tiers.
- Usage-based billing for blockchain transactions.

### Phase 3: Human-AI Interface (PhD Goal)
- **SaulLM-7B Integration:** Plain language summaries of on-chain data.
- **Audit Trails:** Exportable certificates of authenticity for court use (Section 65B Evidence Act).
